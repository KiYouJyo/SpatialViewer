# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want Spatial Viewer to remain a friendly, professional, and respectful environment for contributors with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss facts, code, and reproducible problems constructively.
- Respect different technical approaches and workflows.
- Accept good-faith feedback and separate technical disagreement from personal judgment.
- Respect privacy, copyright, licensing, and confidentiality boundaries around engineering data.
- Provide accurate and verifiable information when reporting problems where practical.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, malicious disclosure of personal or project-sensitive information, deliberate disruption, and other behavior that clearly interferes with healthy collaboration are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove content that violates this policy and may restrict participation when necessary. Security vulnerabilities must follow [SECURITY.en.md](SECURITY.en.md); do not publish exploitable details publicly.
